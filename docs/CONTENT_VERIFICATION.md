## 3. Deliberately Withheld Legacy Claims (Zero Tolerance for Unsubstantiated Data)

To protect the organization's credibility, the following claims from the legacy website are **explicitly excluded** from the rebuild until audited evidence and dates are provided:

1. **"Thousands of lives impacted"**: Excluded. Replaced with honest qualitative descriptions of actual project activities.
2. **"Over 430 children receive food"**: Excluded. No date, school name, or verification record exists.
3. **Sensationalized Mathare descriptions**: Replaced with respectful, dignity-centered language highlighting community resilience and youth talents.
4. **"Classes keep youth away from crime and drugs"**: Excluded as an unverified causal claim.
5. **Invented stats, counters, and progress bars**: Strictly prohibited across all pages.
6. **Fake quotes and star ratings**: Strictly prohibited. The testimonials section remains hidden until genuine written consent is secured.

## 4. Safeguarding & Consent Framework

- **Child Protection**: No full names, home locations, school schedules, or vulnerable family details of children may ever appear on the website.
- **Image Permissions**: Real photographs must have documented consent. For minors under 18, written consent from a parent or guardian is required.
- **Takedown Commitment**: Stated in the Gallery and Privacy Policy: if any community member requests removal of their photo or story, ABWA will remove it promptly.
